The way I work

Follow the evidence.
Challenge the story.

A dashboard is the output, not the investigation. The work starts before the first visual is built.

01

Question

Define the problem, decision or hypothesis before touching the dataset.

02

Data

Identify the available evidence, its source, structure and limitations.

03

Analysis

Model, query and investigate the data to surface patterns and relationships.

04

Validation

Test the story, challenge assumptions and make uncertainty visible.

05

Decision

Translate evidence into clear findings, frameworks and next actions.
