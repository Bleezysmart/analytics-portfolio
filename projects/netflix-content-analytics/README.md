# Netflix Content Analytics

**Content Library & Acquisition Strategy**

This project explores how Netflix builds its content catalogue using data covering movies and TV shows available between **2008 and 2021**.

## Business questions

- Does Netflix prioritise movies or TV shows?
- How has the catalogue grown over time?
- Which genres dominate?
- Which countries contribute the most content?
- What audiences are primarily targeted?
- Does Netflix prioritise newer releases or older licensed titles?

## Data preparation

The analysis addressed missing values across Director, Cast, Country and Rating fields; removed invalid records; created date fields; separated movie duration from TV-show seasons; and created supporting Genre and Country analysis tables.

An **Acquisition Age** metric classified titles into New Release, Recent, Mid-Age, Legacy and Archive categories.

## Key findings

- Movies account for **6,130 titles (69.7%)** of the catalogue.
- TV Shows account for **2,666 titles (30.3%)**.
- International Movies was the largest genre category with **2,752 titles**, followed by Dramas and Comedies.
- The United States contributed **3,688 titles**, followed by India (**1,046**) and the UK (**804**).
- TV-MA was the largest rating group with **3,207 titles**, followed by TV-14 with **2,160**.
- New Releases accounted for **5,554 titles (63.1%)** of the catalogue.

## Recommendations

The source report recommends continued investment in recently released content, continued development of international content, exploring additional family and children's programming, and greater diversification of content sources.
