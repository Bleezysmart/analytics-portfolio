# Furniture Sales Performance Analysis

**Excel Business Intelligence & Profitability Case Study**

A US furniture retail division needed to understand its sales footprint, operational profitability and logistical performance after experiencing significant margin compression in specific product categories.

## Business questions

- What were cumulative sales, profit and item volume?
- How did performance change year over year?
- What seasonal sales patterns emerged?
- Which sub-categories generated strong sales but weak profit?
- What were the shipment-duration patterns?
- Which delivery modes were most used?
- Which cities represented the strongest revenue hubs?
- How did performance vary by customer segment and region?

## Data preparation

More than **2,100 transaction records** were processed in Excel. The workflow included duplicate/null auditing, shipping-duration calculations, categorical binning, temporal normalisation and financial formatting.

## Key findings

- **$742.0K** cumulative revenue.
- **8.0K** units moved.
- Revenue increased **8% YoY**.
- Item volume increased **11% YoY**.
- Net profit fell **57% YoY to $18.5K**.
- Tables and Bookcases generated combined net losses of more than **$21.1K** despite substantial sales volume.
- **50.3%** of shipments clustered around a 4–5 day delivery window.
- New York City led revenue at approximately **$75.7K**, followed by Los Angeles at **$54.0K**.

## Recommendations

The report recommends tightening margin thresholds and discounting on Tables, using cross-selling to support higher-margin categories, and reallocating supply-chain resources around high-value cities.
