# Wexford & Rowan — Q3 Retail Performance Analysis

**Excel Business Intelligence Case Study · Q3 2025**

Wexford & Rowan wanted to understand Q3 retail performance and identify revenue drivers, customer patterns, regional trends and opportunities for Q4.

## Business questions

- What were total Q3 revenue, orders and items sold?
- Which month performed strongest?
- Which weekdays generated the most and least sales?
- Which product categories drove revenue?
- Which states performed strongest and weakest?
- Which customer age group generated the most orders?
- Which period of the day had the highest activity?
- Which payment methods were most preferred?

## Data preparation

The dataset was processed with Excel Power Query to:

- Remove duplicates.
- Standardise customer-region formatting.
- Validate data types and categories.
- Remove a record with a missing customer region.
- Create date fields for monthly and weekday analysis.

## Key results

- Approximately **$264K Q3 revenue**
- **2,000 orders**
- **4,179 items sold**
- **$132 average order value**
- Fashion, Home & Furniture, and Electronics collectively contributed **63% of Q3 revenue**.
- September recorded a **30% increase** compared with the previous month.
- The **26–40** age group represented the largest share of orders.
- Evening shopping generated the highest order volume.

## Business value

The dashboard gives management a concise view of Q3 performance while allowing performance to be explored by product, customer, geography, time and payment behaviour.
