# ASEAN VitalSigns

**Public Health Decision-Making Across ASEAN · SDG 3**

ASEAN VitalSigns is a public-health analytics project developed by Pod Vega to integrate historical health indicators into a unified Power BI decision-support solution.

## Business problem

Healthcare information across ASEAN is distributed across multiple datasets, making it difficult to obtain a unified view of health outcomes, disease burden, healthcare investment, workforce capacity and child health.

The project asks:

- How does healthcare expenditure and workforce capacity relate to life expectancy?
- How do childhood nutrition and immunization relate to infant and under-five mortality?
- Which countries experience higher burdens of communicable diseases?
- Which countries require greater policy attention?
- How can historical health data support healthcare planning and resource allocation?

## Analytical approach

The project integrates multiple healthcare datasets and applies:

- Data cleaning and standardisation
- Dimensional modelling
- DAX calculations
- Interactive filtering and synchronized slicers
- Comparative dashboard visualisation
- Evidence-based interpretation

## Key findings

- Overall health outcomes improved across ASEAN, with life expectancy increasing while infant and maternal mortality declined over the study period.
- Communicable disease burdens vary considerably by country rather than following one uniform regional pattern.
- Significant disparities remain in healthcare investment and workforce availability.
- Regional progress toward SDG 3 is measurable, but country-level gaps remain and require targeted intervention.

## Recommendations

The project supports targeted investment in preventive and maternal healthcare, stronger health-system capacity in lower-capacity countries, and continued monitoring of country-level disparities.
