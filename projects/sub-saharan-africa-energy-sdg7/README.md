# Sub-Saharan Africa Energy Access & Sustainability — SDG 7

**Powering Sub-Saharan Africa**

This project examines electricity access, clean cooking, renewable energy, economic capacity, population and environmental indicators across Sub-Saharan Africa in the context of **SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy**.

## Business questions

- Where are the largest electricity-access gaps?
- How does rural access compare with national access?
- Which countries have stronger or weaker clean-cooking access?
- What patterns exist in renewable-energy consumption?
- How do economic and demographic indicators relate to energy access?
- Where should targeted intervention be considered?

## Dashboard structure

The Power BI solution is organised around:

1. **Executive Overview** — regional energy-access snapshot and country comparisons.
2. **Energy Access & Transition** — electricity, rural access, clean cooking and renewable-energy indicators.
3. **Economy & Sustainability** — GDP per capita, population growth, CO₂ emissions and renewable-energy context.

## Key findings

- Electricity access increased across the study period, but rural communities continue to trail national access levels.
- Large disparities remain between countries in both electricity and clean-cooking access.
- The report identifies a general decline in renewable-energy consumption over the study period, pointing to the need for targeted investment in cleaner energy systems.
- Higher-income countries generally show greater capacity for energy infrastructure investment.
- Energy planning needs to account for demographic growth alongside infrastructure and sustainability objectives.
