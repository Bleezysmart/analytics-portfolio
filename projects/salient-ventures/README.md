# Salient Ventures

**Q4 Sales Performance Analysis**

Salient Ventures is an e-commerce sales analytics project focused on understanding Q4 revenue, expenses, profit, customer behaviour, product profitability, regional performance and sales-person performance.

## Business questions

- What were total revenue, expenses, profit and profit margin?
- How did revenue and profit move across Q4 weeks?
- Which state generated the highest profit?
- Which customers contributed the most revenue?
- How did purchasing behaviour differ by gender?
- Which age groups generated the most revenue?
- Which products and categories were most profitable?
- How did sales personnel perform?

## Key results

During Q4, Salient Ventures generated approximately:

- **$115M revenue**
- **$98M expenses**
- **$17M profit**
- **15% profit margin**

The report also records a recovery in Q4 after revenue had declined to approximately $87.6M in Q3.

## Key findings

- Accessories was the most profitable product category.
- Customers aged 18–35 generated the highest revenue.
- Texas recorded the highest regional profit.
- Jonathan was the strongest sales-person performer across the report's revenue, profit and quantity measures.
- The positive profit margin across transactions indicated that the Q4 sales activity remained profitable.

## Recommendations

The project recommends increasing investment in high-performing categories, strengthening regional strategies, and using balanced performance measures that consider revenue, profit and sales volume rather than volume alone.
