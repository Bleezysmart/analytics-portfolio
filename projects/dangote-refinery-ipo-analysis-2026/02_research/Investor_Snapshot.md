# Dangote Refinery IPO Analysis 2026 — Investor Snapshot

## The question

**Is ₦525 fair?**

This project tests the earnings power and assumptions embedded in the Dangote Refinery IPO price rather than attempting to prove a predetermined investment conclusion.

## H1 2026 operating picture

- Revenue: **$13.91B**
- Net profit: **$1.82B**
- EBITDA / operating earnings: **~$2.60B**
- Refinery utilization: **83.6%**
- Throughput: **~585K bpd**
- Gross refining margin: **$24.50/bbl**
- Operating cash flow: **$1.27B**
- Cash: **$4.27B**
- Gross debt: **$5.67B**
- Net debt: **$1.40B**

Utilization and refining economics improved sharply from FY2024 and FY2025. The sustainability of the H1 2026 margin environment is therefore a central valuation question.

## IPO valuation

- Offer price: **₦525/share**
- Post-IPO equity value: **~$47.82B**
- Business value including net debt: **~$49.22B**
- Post-IPO shares: **~124.23B**

### Earnings-power test

Using an explicit **10× EV/EBITDA analyst assumption**:

| Test | Value |
|---|---:|
| Required sustainable operating earnings | **~$4.92B** |
| Annualized H1 2026 operating earnings | **~$5.20B** |
| Headroom | **~$0.28B** |

The annualized H1 figure is a run-rate, **not a forecast**. The relatively small headroom means the valuation remains sensitive to changes in refining margins, utilization, financing costs, taxes and execution.
