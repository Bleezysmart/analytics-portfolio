# Deskify Office Supply

**Business Intelligence & Profitability Analysis**

Deskify Office Supplies Co. needed a more efficient way to monitor product performance, customer behaviour, geographical profitability and operational efficiency as transaction volumes grew.

## Business problem

The previous reporting process relied heavily on Excel-based consolidation and repetitive calculations. Static reports also limited interactive exploration across products, customers, locations, shipping methods and time periods.

The project replaced that reporting workflow with an interactive Power BI Business Intelligence solution.

## Analytical approach

- Audited the transactional dataset with Power Query.
- Built a scalable **Star Schema** with a central Fact Table and supporting dimensions.
- Developed DAX measures for Revenue, Profit, Orders, Customers and Profit Margin.
- Built separate Product Analysis and Customer Analysis dashboard pages.
- Applied a consistent corporate visual theme and business-question-led dashboard structure.

## Analysis areas

### Product performance
Category and product profitability, shipping modes, regional profitability and monthly profit trends.

### Customer performance
Customer segments, regional performance, high-value customers and sub-category purchasing behaviour.

## Strategic recommendations

The original report recommends:

- Increasing investment in high-profit product categories.
- Optimising underperforming product categories.
- Strengthening corporate customer relationships.
- Developing region-specific growth strategies.
- Improving shipping efficiency.
- Protecting high-value customers.
- Using seasonal trends to improve planning.
